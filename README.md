## Content note

The memoir discusses childhood violence, addiction, grief, suicide and suicidal thoughts, family conflict, medical problems, and legal consequences. Read at your own pace. The author’s own content note and support information appear at the beginning of the online reader. This memoir is not medical, psychological, legal, or professional advice.
